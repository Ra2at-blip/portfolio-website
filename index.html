01 · About

Still calibrating, always building.

I'm Rajat, an engineering student at Kantipur Engineering College, Tribhuvan University. I'm drawn to the parts of engineering where theory meets something you can actually touch, test, and break — that's usually where the real learning happens.

Right now I'm focused on building a strong foundation across core engineering concepts and picking up practical, hands-on skills alongside my coursework. I like documenting what I learn, contributing to small projects, and figuring things out by doing rather than just reading about them.
